## Account Security, Safety Guidelines, and Scam Prevention

As the community grows, bad actors inevitably attempt to exploit trusting collectors. Maintaining strict security measures ensures that your hard-earned roster remains safe from phishing attempts and fraudulent schemes.

Staff members inside the **nba heroes discord** will **never** send unsolicited direct messages asking for account passwords, authentication codes, or external payment transfers. Protect your profile by taking the following precautions:

- **Disable Unsolicited Direct Messages:** In your Discord privacy settings for the server, toggle off direct messages from non-friends to eliminate phishing spam.
- **Inspect External Links Carefully:** Never click on links promising "free legendary card packs" or "developer test builds." Official links will always be posted exclusively in read-only announcement boards.
- **Report Impersonators Promptly:** If an account using a moderator’s avatar and display name contacts you privately, use the server’s ticketing system to alert genuine staff immediately.
- **Never Share Screen Shares Containing Auth Tokens:** When streaming your matches or pack openings in community voice channels, ensure your sensitive client credentials remain hidden.

By adhering to these defensive standards, you protect your digital roster and contribute to a healthier, safer environment for every collector on the server.
