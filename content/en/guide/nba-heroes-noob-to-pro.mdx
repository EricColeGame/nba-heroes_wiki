### Shot Meter Discipline
Relying entirely on the visual shot meter can create lag vulnerabilities in fast-paced online lobbies. Pro players turn off or look past the meter, focusing instead on player release animations—such as the apex of the jump or the wrist flick. This small adjustment provides wider shot bonus windows and greater consistency under defensive pressure.

### Defensive Footwork and Cushioning
Novice defenders frequently hold the turbo trigger and lunge for steals, which instantly triggers blown-by blowby animations. Pros practice defensive shadowing:
* Keep a two-step cushion against elite playmakers.
* Shade ball-handlers toward the sideline to cut off half the court.
* Only commit to shot-contest animations once the opponent enters their gathered release motion.
