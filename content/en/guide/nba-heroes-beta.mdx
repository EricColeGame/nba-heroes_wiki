Resource allocation throughout the **NBA Heroes beta** follows a focused economy model. Upgrading your favorite athletes requires deliberate choices between raw physical attributes, offensive craft, and defensive badges.

Investing resources blindly across an entire roster can stall your team's overall progress. Instead, concentrate your upgrade materials on your primary scorer and defensive center first before branching out into role players.

```
Base Athlete Card ➔ Skill Tree Unlock ➔ Gear Socketing ➔ Synergy Awakening
```

1. **Base Attribute Training**: Elevates raw statistics like speed, vertical leap, three-point shot, and interior contest.
2. **Badge Specialization**: Grants situational passives, such as boosting contested jumpers or enhancing interception reach.
3. **Gear Slots**: Equip shoes, wristbands, and knee sleeves that provide minor flat stat enhancements.
4. **Hero Awakening**: Unlocks secondary ultimate triggers once a card reaches maximum training tier.

## Pro Tips for Dominating the Ranked Ladder

Climbing the leaderboards requires more than just high-tier cards. Incorporate these actionable habits into your daily play sessions:

- **Bait the Help Defense**: Drive toward the basket to trigger interior rotations, then kick the ball out to wide-open perimeter shooters.
- **Master the Shot Meter Without Meter**: Disabling the visual shot meter often awards a slight green-window boost once you learn a player's natural release point.
- **Conserve Ultimates for Clutch Moments**: Avoid burning signature abilities in the first quarter; save them for late-game swing possessions or buzzer-beaters.
- **Communicate Defensive Switches**: In multiplayer modes, manual communication prevents two defenders from jumping at the same pump fake.
