### Optimizing Bench Depth and Energy

A common mistake among rising contenders is exhausting their starting five. Stamina depletion reduces defensive contest percentages and increases turnover rates during the fourth quarter. 

* **Rotational Waves:** Maintain a 6th and 7th man who can sustain defensive intensity without dropping offensive spacing.
* **Specialized Closers:** Reserve your highest-rated clutch shooters for the final three minutes to trigger crunch-time synergy buffs.
* **Foul Management:** Keep backup rim protection ready to prevent opponent bonus free throws when your starters get into early foul trouble.
