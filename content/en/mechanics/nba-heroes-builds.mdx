## Core Mechanics: How Builds Function in NBA Heroes

Character progression in the game revolves around balancing physical measurements, raw attribute caps, and badge upgrade slots. Height, weight, and wingspan directly alter your baseline ceilings before you ever spend an attribute token. For instance, lengthening wingspan dramatically improves block ratings and passing lane deflections, but it marginally penalizes your open three-point jumper and ball-handling speed.

Attributes are divided into five core categories: Finishing, Shooting, Playmaking, Defense/Rebounding, and Physicals. Reaching specific attribute thresholds unlocks distinct skill tiers that change your hero's in-game physics. Hitting 85 Ball Handle unlocks elite dribble moves, while an 80 Vertical paired with an 84 Driving Dunk grants access to high-flying posterizer animations.

```
Attribute Allocation -> Measurement Modifiers -> Badge Tier Unlocks -> Elite Animations
```

To optimize your hero, you must avoid spreading skill points too thin across every category. Dedicated specialists and balanced two-way hybrids consistently outperform "jack-of-all-trades" characters that fail to unlock elite badge tiers.

| Stat Category | Primary Focus Attributes | Key Gameplay Breakpoint Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Finishing** | Driving Dunk, Close Shot, Layup | Unlocks contact dunk packages and acrobat layups |
| **Shooting** | 3-Point Shot, Mid-Range, Free Throw | Accelerates shot meter green windows and off-dribble accuracy |

## Step-by-Step Leveling and Progression Strategy

Grinding your player from a rookie prospect to a maxed-out hero requires strategic allocation of your earned currency and skill points. Misallocating early resources can make early matchmaking a frustrating slog.

Follow this progression sequence to keep your player competitive at every stage of the journey:

1. **Max Physical Attributes First:** Speed, Stamina, and Acceleration dictate your baseline responsiveness. Upgrading these early ensures you keep pace with higher-tier players on fast breaks.
2. **Hit Your Primary Scoring Threshold:** If you are a shooter, push Three-Point Shot to at least 85 immediately to unlock reliable green windows. If you are a slasher, secure your Driving Dunk packages first.
3. **Upgrade Core Defensive Attributes:** Perimeter Defense and Steal (or Rebound and Block for centers) provide immediate XP gains through defensive stops, rebounds, and deflections.
4. **Unlock Ball Handling & Playmaking:** Raise Ball Handle to your desired dribble package threshold so you can reliably create your own shot off the bounce.
5. **Round Out Secondary Stats:** Finish your build by investing in Free Throw, Mid-Range, and Interior Defense to eliminate weak points in your matchup profile.

Consistently practicing your jump-shot timing in solo shootaround modes will cement your build's muscle memory, turning theoretical stat advantages into real match-winning performances.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best overall archetype among NBA Heroes builds for beginners?
The Glass-Cleaning Stretch Big is the most forgiving build for new players. It offers an easy learning curve because it focuses on fundamental responsibilities: securing defensive rebounds, protecting the paint, and spacing out to the corner for open catch-and-shoot jumpers. You can make an immediate, positive impact on your team without needing to master complex dribble combinations.

### How much do physical attributes like wingspan impact shooting accuracy?
Wingspan plays a major role in your final attribute caps. Increasing your wingspan lowers your open three-point and mid-range shooting caps by several points while raising your contest, block, and steal ratings. If you plan to build a pure sharpshooter, keep your wingspan near default or slightly reduced to preserve the highest possible shooting ratings and largest green release windows.

### Can you modify or respec your attributes after finalizing a build?
In most competitive modes, base attribute caps and physical measurements (height, weight, wingspan) are locked once you finalize your character creation. While you can reallocate unlocked badge points and swap out active skills freely between games, changing your core attribute distribution usually requires creating a new player slot. Plan your target attribute breakpoints carefully before committing your hard-earned progression points.

### Why are badge tiers more important than raw attributes in NBA Heroes builds?
Raw attributes only set your baseline mathematical success rate, whereas badges unlock unique animations, ignore defensive contest percentages, and significantly widen your shot meter timing windows. A player with an 85 Three-Point Shot and Hall of Fame shooting badges will consistently outshoot a player with a 95 rating who has no complementary badges equipped. Building around badge tier thresholds is essential for peak performance.
