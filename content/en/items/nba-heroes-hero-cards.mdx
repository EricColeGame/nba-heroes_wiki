## Gameplay Strategies and Tactical Tips

Veteran managers often rotate their **nba heroes hero cards** depending on their opponent's defensive schemes. Dominating online modes requires adjusting in real time to your opponent's tendencies.

### Exploiting Archetype Matchups

- **When facing heavy zone defense:** Deploy cards equipped with the *Deadly Distance* perk. Overload one side of the floor to force recovery animations, leaving open catch-and-shoot corners.
- **When defending transition-heavy teams:** Sub in high-agility perimeter defenders. Keep manual control over your guard to bump ball-handlers before they reach full sprint speed.
- **Managing Late-Game Moments:** In clutch situations, ensure your primary hero card possesses badges that mitigate fatigue penalties on late-shot-clock attempts.

### Common Roster Mistakes to Avoid

- **Over-relying on a single scorer:** If an opponent isolates your primary scorer with a defensive hero, your offense will stall without a reliable secondary creator.
- **Ignoring badge synergy:** Equipping offensive shooting badges onto a pure interior rebounder wastes valuable slot potential. Play strictly to each card's innate archetype design.
- **Neglecting card durability and stamina:** A tired superstar suffers severe attribute penalties, leading to missed open shots and defensive blow-bys.
